Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: we collect as little as we can, we tell you when your text or voice is sent to a server, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains how Ebru Gelebek (“we”, “us”), based in Ankara, Türkiye, handles information in our iPhone and iPad apps, including ttsAI (text to speech) and our speech-to-text app, and on this website.

1. What we process

  • Your content. In ttsAI: text, documents, web pages, and photos or scans you ask the app to read, summarize or translate. In our speech-to-text app: audio you record for transcription.
  • Identifiers. An app or device identifier and a subscription profile ID, used to run the service, apply usage limits and provide support.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Basic product analytics and crash reports.
  • Purchase status. Whether you have an active subscription. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details.
  • Messages to us. Your email address and what you write if you contact support.

We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t collect your contacts, precise location or health data.

2. When your content leaves your device

On-device by default where possible. Our speech-to-text app can transcribe using your device’s built-in speech recognition, which works offline. In that mode your audio stays on your phone.

Server processing, only for the features that need it. The following send your content to our servers, and from there to third-party AI providers that generate the result (currently including Google’s Gemini models):

  • ttsAI: premium voices, summaries, translations, and converting PowerPoint files.
  • Speech-to-text app: AI transcription.

We ask for your permission before this happens for the first time. We use your content only to produce the result you requested, not to advertise to you, and we do not sell it. Providers process it on our behalf under their own terms and are not permitted by us to use it for anything else.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and secure the apps.
  • To apply fair-use limits and manage subscriptions.
  • To understand which features are used and fix crashes.
  • To answer your messages and meet legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the apps: Apple (App Store and payments), Adapty (subscription management), Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), and AI providers as described above. They receive only what they need to do their job. We don’t use your data for cross-app tracking, and the apps don’t show third-party ads.

5. Retention

Content sent for processing is kept only as long as needed to return your result and operate the service, then deleted. Content saved in your library stays on your device until you delete it. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period. Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you.

6. Your choices and rights

You can delete your saved items inside the app at any time, use on-device options where available, and stop using the apps whenever you like. Depending on where you live, including under the EU GDPR and Türkiye’s KVKK (Law No. 6698), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us to make a request and we’ll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security and transfers

We use encrypted connections and reasonable safeguards. Because our providers operate internationally, your information may be processed outside your country, including outside Türkiye and the EU, with appropriate protections where the law requires them.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.

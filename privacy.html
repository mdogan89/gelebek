Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ebru Gelebek (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a mobile app company based in Ankara, Türkiye. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website, services, or apps.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect or store personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal data. The content you submit to the app (such as text, documents, images, or audio) is used only to generate the result you requested and is deleted from our servers as soon as processing is complete.

2. How we use your information

Because we do not store your information, the only use we make of the content you submit is to:

  • Generate the result you requested (for example, text-to-speech conversion) and deliver it to you
  • Respond to you if you contact us directly
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process personal data based on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, or legal obligation, as appropriate.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share your personal data, prompts, images, or other content you submit for AI features with third parties. We process this content on our own servers for general AI operations, such as text-to-speech conversion, and deliver the result to you. Once the processing is complete, we delete this content from our servers. We never share your data with third parties.

4,5. Face Data

We do not store your facial data in any way. After the AI processing is completed and the result is generated, once we show you the product using the photos you provided, we permanently delete your data.

5. Data retention

We do not retain your data. Content you submit for processing is deleted from our servers immediately after the result is delivered to you. Content you save inside the app stays only on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

6. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Depending on where you live, you may still have rights under data protection law, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, to data portability, and to object or withdraw consent. If you believe we hold any data about you, contact us and we will respond.

7. Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. Our apps do not show third-party ads and do not use cross-app tracking.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at ebrugelebek125@gmail.com or Ebru Gelebek, Ankara, Türkiye.

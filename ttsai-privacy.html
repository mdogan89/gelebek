This Privacy Policy applies to the ttsAI mobile application, provided by Ebru Gelebek (“we”, “us”, “our”), a mobile app company based in Ankara, Türkiye. It describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use ttsAI.

1. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact and identity information (e.g. name, email, company) when you get in touch or use our services

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

3. Legal basis (KVKK)

Where applicable under Turkish law, including the Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK, Law No. 6698), we process personal data based on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, or legal obligation, as appropriate.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share your personal data, prompts, or other content you submit for AI features with third parties. We process this content on our own servers for general AI operations, such as text-to-speech conversion, and deliver the result to you. Once the processing is complete, we delete this content from our servers. We never share your data with third parties.

5. Data retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, to data portability, and to object or withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at ebrugelebek125@gmail.com or Ebru Gelebek, Ankara, Türkiye.
